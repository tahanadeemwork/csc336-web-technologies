LINEN CAMP SHIRT

Rs. 6,450.00

Description

A relaxed short-sleeve camp shirt in breathable linen, with an open collar and a straight hem. Easy to wear on its own or layered.

The fabric is woven from European flax, which gives the shirt its natural texture and keeps it cool on warm days. Linen softens with every wash, so the shirt feels better the longer you own it.

The cut is intentionally roomy through the chest and shoulders. The camp collar lies flat when worn open and sits neatly when the top button is fastened. A straight hem means it can be worn untucked without looking untidy.

Small details make the difference: corozo-style buttons, reinforced side seams, and a single chest pocket that is stitched twice so it holds its shape. The inside seams are finished neatly so nothing scratches against the skin.

Style it with tailored trousers for the office, or with shorts for a weekend outing. In cooler weather, wear it open over a plain t-shirt. The neutral colour works with almost everything in a wardrobe.

Sizing: the model is 6 feet tall and wears a medium. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one for a looser drape, or the smaller one for a closer fit. All measurements are taken flat.

Care: machine wash cold with similar colours and do not bleach. Dry flat or tumble dry on low. Iron on a medium setting while the shirt is slightly damp for the smoothest finish. Light creasing is part of the character of linen.

Delivery and returns: orders above Rs. 2500 ship free. Unworn items can be exchanged within seven days of delivery, with the original tags attached. Contact customer service for help with any order.
